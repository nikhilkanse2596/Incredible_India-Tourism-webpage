NOHKALIKAI FALLS


"The beauty of Nohkalikai Falls, located in Cherrapunji, Meghalaya, is its dramatic, awe-inspiring height and its haunting, evocative pool.
As India's tallest plunge waterfall, the water drops a staggering 340 meters (over 1,100 feet) in a single, powerful cascade down a rugged cliff face. Below, the water collects in a deep, circular pool that is famous for its striking turquoise-blue or emerald-green color, contrasting vividly with the surrounding lush, mist-shrouded green forests.
It is a truly spectacular sight, embodying a blend of nature's raw power and a serene, jewel-toned isolation."

located in East Khasi Hills district, Meghalaya, India

DUDHSAGAR FALLS


The beauty of Dudhsagar Falls is its sheer, dramatic power and milky-white splendor.
Cascading over 300 meters in four tiers through the lush, dense forests of the Western Ghats (on the Goa-Karnataka border), the volume of water creates an immense white spray and foam. This phenomenal sight truly lives up to its name, which translates to the "Sea of Milk". The backdrop of the deep green, rich tropical forest, often with a passing train visible in the distance, makes it a majestic, wild, and unforgettable spectacle of nature's force.

LODH FALLS


The beauty of Lodh Falls (also known as Budha Ghagh) lies in its majestic height and deep forest seclusion in Jharkhand.
As the highest waterfall in Jharkhand, the water of the Burha River plunges a tremendous 143 meters (about 469 feet) in multiple tiers, creating a thunderous spectacle often audible from miles away. This raw, powerful cascade is set amidst the dense, pristine woodlands of the Chota Nagpur Plateau, offering a serene, yet exhilarating, natural escape.
It is a striking symbol of wilderness grandeur and powerful tranquility.

located in Latehar, Jharkhand, India
located near Shillong, Meghalaya, India

SWEET FALLS


Sweet Falls, located in Shillong, Meghalaya, is beautiful for its blend of sheer, dramatic drop and secluded, untouched wilderness.
The waterfall plunges a stunning 96 meters (over 315 feet) in a single, unbroken stream, making it one of the steepest in the area. This powerful white cascade is beautifully framed by lush green hills and thick, tranquil forest, giving it an atmosphere of majestic serenity and raw, untamed nature.
