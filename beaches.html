RUSHIKONDA BEACH


"Rushikonda Beach, the "Jewel of the East Coast" in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, is beautiful for its picturesque harmony.
It features pristine golden sands and clear turquoise waters of the Bay of Bengal, dramatically framed by lush green hills and palm groves. This serene, clean environment, which has earned it a Blue Flag Certification, makes it a perfect blend of natural tranquility and a vibrant hub for water sports.
It embodies coastal serenity backed by mountainous green grandeur."

PURI BEACH


"Puri Beach, also known as the Golden Beach, is beautiful because it is where spirituality meets the sea.
It is a long, vibrant stretch of golden sand on the Bay of Bengal, famous for its breathtaking sunrises. What makes it unique is the constant presence of spiritual significance: pilgrims take a purifying dip in the holy water before visiting the nearby Jagannath Temple, and the air is often filled with the rhythm of crashing waves mingling with hymns. It is also a living art gallery, famed for the intricate sand sculptures created by local artists.
It embodies a blend of sacred devotion and vibrant coastal life."

located in Puri, Odisha, India

KOLLAM BEACH


"The beauty of Kollam Beach (also known as Mahatma Gandhi Beach) lies in its historical charm and gentle serenity on Kerala's coastline.
It features a long stretch of clean, golden sand and the calming rhythm of the Arabian Sea. The scenic view is dominated by the historic Thangasseri Lighthouse, whose presence adds a timeless, historic character to the sunset vista. As the gateway to the famous Kerala backwaters, it offers a beautiful coastal transition from the vast sea to tranquil inland waters.
It is a peaceful blend of maritime heritage and classic coastal tranquility."

ALIBAUG


"The beauty of Alibaug Beach is its dramatic backdrop of history and nature.
It is famous for its stretches of dark, often black, sand that contrast uniquely with the clear waters of the Arabian Sea. What truly defines its beauty is the constant, breathtaking view of the Kolaba Fort, a centuries-old Maratha sea fortress that sits majestically in the ocean, creating a stunning silhouette, especially during the famous sunrise and sunset.
It is a striking blend of coastal history and scenic, rustic charm."

located in Maharashtra, India
